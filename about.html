About SALT

Hello, I'm SALT.

An independent marketing and design studio backed by more than 7 years of hands-on marketing experience across corporate, agency and independent client work.

Why SALT?

Marketing should add something.

“You are the salt of the earth.”Matthew 5:13

SALT reflects the belief that we are called to bring goodness, purpose and positive influence into the spaces we occupy. That meaning sits quietly at the heart of the business and the way I choose to work: with integrity, purpose, kindness and intention.

From a marketing perspective, the same principle applies. Salt doesn't need to be the biggest ingredient to make a difference. Neither does your brand.

The single salt grain within the SALT identity represents exactly that: a small detail capable of changing the whole, much like the intentional choices that make a brand distinctive, memorable and worth noticing.
